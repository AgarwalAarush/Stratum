# Stratum investment-pipeline audit

Read-only audit, 6 September 2026. Evidence collected beginning approximately 07:40 UTC. No code, Git branches, database records, worker configuration, schedules, or deployments were changed. Audit files and a source copy were created only under `/private/tmp`.

## 1. Readiness verdict

**Not ready for dependable daily investment recommendations.** Stratum has substantial research infrastructure, but it is not yet an operating, measurable portfolio recommendation system. The immediate production blocker is Supabase service restriction. Independently of that outage, the recommendation publisher and investment-outcome learning loop are missing, portfolio evidence is inconsistent across consumers, and World-to-company handoffs lose important context.

This is not a recommendation to rewrite Stratum. Preserve its normalized market data, immutable World evidence, worker queue, company research, thesis review, and portfolio infrastructure. Build the missing decision contract through those components.

### Freshness, scheduling, and reliability

`agent-schedule.ts` schedules asset sync daily; screener/cross assets in market-refresh windows; FMP at market-clock-dependent cadence; thesis monitoring at 5 minutes in-window; portfolio research around 06:10 ET; legacy thesis cycles and World Thinker around 06:00/18:00 ET; benchmark refresh Sunday 20:00 ET; brokerage at 09:20, 12:15, 16:15, 20:00 ET on weekdays. Worker polling defaults to 5 seconds and scheduling/heartbeat to 60 seconds. Actual successful completion of those schedules is unverified.

Daily deterministic dedupe keys, SQL `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, attempt limits, exponential job retry, stale/interrupted-job recovery and queue coalescing are implemented. Narrow ten-minute schedule windows can be missed across downtime; there is no durable business-date completion contract ensuring every daily investment obligation eventually completes. Several schedules use weekday/time logic rather than a complete exchange calendar; the screener also checks Alpaca's clock. A pipeline deadline should use actual trading sessions, holidays, half days and feed latency.

The worker currently retries the global database restriction at its normal poll interval. Recovery fails before scheduling and processing can proceed. `processOneAgentJob` also awaits terminal update requests without checking their returned Supabase `error` values, so execution and durable completion can diverge. These facts argue for an independent health monitor, classified retry policy and explicit publication acknowledgments.

Atomic screener publication protects against partially written rows, but its SQL only rejects **zero** rows. It does not prove required-universe coverage, minimum freshness or correct distribution of missing names. Official holdings parsing has minimum counts and last-complete fallback, yet returned quote/history coverage can still shrink before publication. Asset sync only upserts returned active/tradable assets; it does not deactivate previously stored names absent from the latest response.

Cache ownership is largely sensible: Supabase is durable; Redis and process caches accelerate read models; World raw evidence/Git have worker-local durability. However, `composeLatestMarketOverview` combines a specified equity snapshot with the **latest** cross-asset, leadership, candidate and fallback memo records. That is not a time-coherent historical snapshot. Readers may return null on DB errors and the overview route may use explicitly labeled illustrative fallback; a successful HTTP response therefore proves little. This audit did not invoke application routes that could populate caches or enqueue work.

Retention defaults are 7 days for nonlatest market snapshots, 30 days for cross assets and completed jobs; memo-referenced equity snapshots are protected. Deleting jobs cascades to runs; market snapshot deletion cascades to rows/state. Memo protection can also retain large snapshots indefinitely. The quota cause cannot be attributed to a particular table without database measurements. Decision evidence needs its own compact retention guarantee, rather than retaining every intraday universe snapshot indefinitely or deleting the only reproducible input.

## 3. Ranked broken and missing links

### P0 — blocks operational or product readiness

1. **Production durable data plane is restricted.** HTTP 402 plus worker errors is direct evidence. Restore capacity/service through a separately authorized operational task, measure table sizes/egress, and prove successful publications. An alive daemon or READY deployment does not mitigate this. Do not assume Friday's quotes or positions exist.

2. **No daily recommendation contract or publication gate.** `thesis_decisions` is owner-authored; equity research's actions are `buy_now`, `nibble`, `wait`, `add_on_weakness`, `avoid`, and disposition is `own/watch/avoid`. Those are useful fragments but do not distinguish every requested portfolio action or ensure a daily decision for each holding. Before actionable recommendations, require fresh evidence, source validation, explicit risks/counter-thesis/horizons, portfolio context, and a reasoned no-trade path. [Current decision contract](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/markets/types.ts#L923).

### P1 — materially changes recommendation correctness or measurability

3. **Portfolio ownership has conflicting sources of truth.** `fetchPortfolioWorkspace` uses successful broker snapshots and excludes voided transactions. `fetchPortfolioResearchCoverage`, Candidate Scout tracking and `scanResearchRefreshes` reconstruct ownership from transaction rows without the same void filtering or broker override. World dependencies inherit research coverage. A broker-only holding can therefore be missing from research/risk coverage; corrected or voided trades can create phantom exposure. The live incidence is unverified, but the divergence is implemented. [Coverage input](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/portfolio-research-seeding.ts#L124), [monitor input](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/research-monitoring.ts#L31).

4. **The causal handoff loses semantics.** World leads store transmission, capture conditions, decisive questions, contradictions and source IDs, and enqueue a job with originating commit/node IDs. But the company-research call receives symbol, owner, reason and only an optional legacy market-thesis ID. It does not pass/load that World dossier before research. A reverse research-to-World feedback path exists after completion. The legacy causal projection also reads `content.economics` as an object and `content.sourceIds`, whereas promotion writes economics as a string plus `economicCapture` and `sourceLedger`; specialized projected fields can be empty. `owner_review_items` investigation is only a status update. [Job handoff](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/agent-jobs.ts#L936), [projection](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/causal-model.ts#L113), [producer](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/world-memory.ts#L580).

5. **A completed report is not verified investment evidence.** `CompanyPacket` accepts empty optional statements, SEC/transcript/segment/external-evidence results and still stores `status: complete`. FMP sources use retrieval time; packet `dataAsOf` is the newest among selected heterogeneous timestamps, not a guarantee that all decisive inputs are fresh. The final research validator checks structure/word count but not source membership or section-to-ledger consistency. A pure local probe accepted nonexistent source IDs. The writer silently filters source rows against packet membership. The prompt forces a directional BUY/HOLD/SELL on thin packets; `NOT_RATED` is rejected by the validator. Keep directional opinion separate from permission to act. [Packet](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/company-research.ts#L301), [validator](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/company-research.ts#L582), [publication](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/company-research.ts#L739).

6. **Replay cannot establish historical investment performance.** Historical event selection filters publication dates but not original availability; gaps may be filled by live web search. `runWorldThinker` retrieves the current World branch, active asset registry, current portfolio, signals and coverage, with no historical decision cutoff. Replay defaults to the configured World branch, and `canonicalProjection: false` does not isolate all event/projection state. It is reconstruction/replay, not a point-in-time investment backtest. Even perfect runtime filtering would not remove hindsight embedded in a modern LLM's training. [Replay](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/world-replay.ts#L155), [current-context retrieval](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/world-thinker.ts#L326), [historical windows](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/world-events.ts#L570).

7. **Investment outcomes are neither immutable nor measured.** `decision_reviews` is unique by owner/decision and is upserted, overwriting prior outcome/lessons/postmortem. Any review after a decision permanently suppresses that version's future 90-day review; the pure-function probe confirms this. Market prediction evaluation is useful but uses the first bounded set of linked observations ingested after thesis generation, with no explicit upper outcome cutoff and no guarantee of balanced contradictory evidence. There are no recommendation-level markouts, benchmarks, execution links or confidence calibration. [Review upsert](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/portfolio.ts#L796), [review scheduling](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/markets/capital-allocation.ts#L181), [prediction evidence](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/server/market-prediction-evaluation.ts#L81).

8. **Data quality and risk checks are descriptive, not a complete action gate.** Screener publication lacks required-coverage checks. Capital checks use owner-entered classifications as a proxy for correlated exposure and current-session volume for exit liquidity, not a factor exposure model or average daily liquidity. Unknown inputs can remain warnings and `record_capital_decision` stores blocked assessments; this is a review record, not enforced authorization. Many thesis kill criteria are prose; automated decision alerts implement only price criteria. Stale evidence can coexist with a new generation timestamp. [Snapshot gate](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/supabase/migrations/202607150001_markets_core.sql#L134), [capital checks](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/markets/capital-allocation.ts#L60), [monitoring](https://github.com/AgarwalAarush/Stratum/blob/e06dff1d8a7303f530a2c532abe1ac1f19f24b31/lib/markets/monitoring.ts#L21).

9. **Retention and backups do not establish reproducibility.** World evidence has strong immutability triggers, but current universe membership, adjusted bar upserts, ordinary feed records and overwritten reviews are not a complete bitemporal archive. Default job/cross-asset retention is shorter than many thesis horizons. Corpus backup support exists but is not configured in the active worker environment. Freeze compact decision inputs and prove a restore before relying on old artifacts.

### P2 — quality and efficiency after the critical path

10. **Confidence and score semantics need discipline.** Candidate `attentionPriority` combines fundamentals, momentum, selloff urgency and tracking bonuses, but is used for investigation ordering rather than a buy rating. That separation is good. Market-state confidence is a breadth heuristic; legacy hypothesis confidence is partly evidence-count based; research confidence is model supplied. None has demonstrated probabilistic calibration. Keep these meanings separate and expose dimensions rather than promoting any aggregate to conviction or size.

11. **Breadth and routing benchmarks overstate neither coverage nor alpha.** Sixteen news topics, source URLs, route precision/recall and many journal commits measure different things. The World benchmark samples already admitted events, so it cannot measure important events absent from ingestion. Use independent missed-event sampling, primary-release coverage and portfolio-mechanism coverage. Do not add more agents or sources before measuring conversion, missed obligations and owner review time.

## 4. Target architecture within the existing stack

Add a typed `DecisionContext` assembled on macserver from existing storage. It must reference a frozen input manifest: World commit/causal versions, governed evidence, market snapshot, source-specific macro observations, company packet/model/research, accepted or invalidated thesis version, and authoritative portfolio snapshot. Store a content hash, decision cutoff, data-quality report and calculation/model/schema versions. Do not requery “latest” after freezing.

Use existing `agent_jobs` with additional job types: `assemble-decision-context`, `generate-daily-recommendations`, `evaluate-recommendation-markouts`, `evaluate-recommendation-thesis`, and `review-recommendation-cohort`. These names are proposals, not existing jobs. Use deterministic owner/date/session/policy dedupe keys and a durable dependency/completion manifest with catch-up. Add an independent read-only health monitor for stale publications and database restriction; do not make the unavailable database the only location where failure can be detected.

Decision generation compares credible alternatives: act, wait, maintain, reduce risk, research, or reject. Separate company quality, causal thesis, expectations/valuation, entry timing, and portfolio fit. A structured critic tests contradictions, missing decisive facts and price/source validity. A deterministic validator enforces evidence membership, freshness, bounds, portfolio consistency and risk rules. Publish the complete batch and outcome schedules atomically. Vercel reads published batches; the owner records intent/override separately. Brokerage access stays read-only.

Do not require an accepted bullish thesis to recommend a justified trim/sell after invalidation. Link the old thesis and the invalidating evidence. New risk requires sufficient affirmative support; a risk-reduction action has a different evidence contract. A data-outage no-trade must say that evaluation failed; it must not silently claim that existing positions are safe to hold.

## 5. Recommendation ledger and outcome-learning design

### Proposed immutable schema

Use additive Supabase tables and existing artifact IDs. Keep large raw payloads in the corpus/object archive, with immutable hashes and tested backups. Published records reject UPDATE/DELETE; mutable processing state lives separately. Corrections, cancellations, overrides and evaluations append events or new versions. Foreign keys should preserve decision history when assets are delisted or operational jobs are pruned.

| Proposed table | Required record |
|---|---|
| `recommendation_batches` | Owner, portfolio scope, trading date/session, decision cutoff, generated/published times, revision, policy/schema/code/model versions, input-manifest ID/hash, required universe, coverage, missing inputs, publication result. One complete daily batch; explicit failure/abstention when needed. |
| `recommendation_versions` | Immutable ID, recommendation family/episode ID, version, supersedes ID, batch ID, stable security ID plus symbol-at-time, action, action reason, horizon and expiry, thesis/research/model/World references, thesis and counter-thesis snapshots, expected mechanism and priced-expectations claim, decisive questions, catalysts, risks, falsifiers, confidence forecasts, source manifest, portfolio context and proposed exposure change. |
| `recommendation_input_manifests` / `recommendation_evidence` | Exact input artifact versions and values; source ID/URL/content hash; event/effective period, publisher release time, first observed/available time, ingested time, retrieved time, revision/vintage, feed, calculation window and units. Preserve missingness and observation quality. |
| `recommendation_forecasts` | Specific observable proposition, target metric/threshold, deadline, probability, confirmation and invalidation criteria, permissible sources, benchmark/peer selection and evaluation policy fixed at issuance. Narrative confidence remains a different field. |
| `recommendation_owner_events` | Presented, acknowledged, accepted, rejected, delayed, modified, manually executed or cancelled; owner rationale, intended/actual action and size, event time, related version and transaction IDs. Append corrections. Never infer acceptance from a brokerage position change alone. |
| `recommendation_markouts` | Version, horizon, evaluation-as-of, eligible reference price/time/feed, endpoint price and corporate-action treatment, gross/net return, benchmark/peer/exposure-relative returns, MFE/MAE, drawdown, risk metrics, data quality and evaluator version. | `recommendation_thesis_assessments` | Forecast ID, observation cutoff, confirmation/disconfirmation/inconclusive/not-yet-due/expired-without-evidence, supporting and contrary evidence, observed mechanism, reason code, evaluator/critic/owner adjudication. |
| `recommendation_attributions` / `recommendation_cohort_reviews` | Versioned selection/timing/sizing/risk/owner-difference attribution, fixed comparison policies, uncertainty, root-cause labels, proposed process change, training/evaluation cohort IDs, shadow experiment and promotion decision. |

Action semantics must be explicit: **research** seeks an answer; **watch** defines a trigger; **buy** opens exposure; **add** increases it; **hold** affirmatively maintains existing exposure; **trim** partially reduces it; **sell** closes it; **no_trade** explicitly abstains with reason and reassessment trigger. Formal BUY/HOLD/SELL research ratings remain separate. A no-trade due to insufficient data is not interchangeable with an affirmative hold.

Entry/exit contract: reference quote and timestamp, executable session, expiry, entry range or condition, maximum acceptable price, valuation assumptions, cost/slippage assumptions, target exposure/size range and limits, catalyst/earnings constraints, fundamental invalidation and risk exit rules. Never turn a price stop into proof that the economic thesis is false. If meaningful sizing is unavailable, mark it unavailable and restrict the recommendation accordingly.

Portfolio snapshot: account/version, broker/ledger provenance, timestamp, cash, holdings, cost basis if relevant, current/proposed weights, sector/factor/causal exposures, liquidity needs and constraints. Store an immutable copy or durable reference. Distinguish recommendation, owner intent and actual fill; unmatched or ambiguous fills remain unlinked.

The eligible-universe/candidate manifest must retain selected, rejected, suppressed and unevaluable names plus selection reasons. Otherwise the ledger will measure only surviving published ideas. Include explicit no-trade and unresolved cases. Multiple daily versions of one thesis belong to one episode for statistical clustering; they are not independent bets.

### Evaluation protocol

**Time zero:** use the actual publication time and first eligible executable quote/session after it, respecting delayed feeds. Never assume an after-close recommendation filled at the earlier close. Maintain separate signal-reference, predeclared model-execution and actual owner-fill evaluations. A conditional entry that never triggers is an unfilled recommendation, not a conveniently priced trade.

**Markouts:** schedule 5, 10 and 20 trading-session checks from publication, plus action/entry checks where applicable and the original thesis horizon. Preserve expired, unfilled, invalidated, delisted and censored cases. Short markouts diagnose timing/risk; they do not grade a multiyear thesis as true or false. Corporate actions, dividends, mergers, currencies and delisting proceeds require explicit treatment; do not double count dividends if adjusted prices already include them.

**Benchmarks:** fix an appropriate broad-market total-return benchmark and a sector/industry or point-in-time matched-peer basket at issuance. Match size, profitability, beta and business exposures when relevant. Record both raw and relative returns; add factor-adjusted analysis only with adequate observations. Use a cash return for genuinely unallocated capital and the unchanged portfolio as the primary counterfactual for hold/trim/sell decisions. Selling before a stock falls is not automatically successful if the funded alternative loses more or risk increases.

**Selection:** compare equal-risk selected names with the eligible-universe/peer alternative using one common execution rule. Include declined/watch/no-trade candidates to measure missed opportunities and avoided risks; shadow comparisons are estimates, not claims about trades the owner would have made.

**Timing:** hold names and baseline sizes fixed; compare actual recommendation entry/exit rules with a simple preregistered baseline, such as the next eligible session and fixed horizon. Include slippage, waiting cash and unfilled orders.

**Sizing:** hold selection and timing fixed; compare recommended weights with an equal-risk/capped baseline, recording incremental portfolio return, drawdown, concentration and cash impact. Evaluate actual owner sizing separately.

**Risk management:** compare explicit trim/exit/invalidation actions with the unchanged-position path, including avoided losses, foregone gains, turnover, liquidity and tail exposure. These attributions interact. Use a fixed sequential decomposition with a residual, or a documented Shapley decomposition later; do not label them experimentally proven causal effects.

**Thesis correctness:** resolve the declared mechanism independently of price. Did demand, capacity, pricing, margin, competition or policy evolve as predicted? Did shareholder economics follow? Did the market simply rerate an unrelated factor? Store “right mechanism/wrong timing,” “wrong mechanism/positive return,” “correct risk reduction,” “insufficient evidence,” and similar reason codes. Gather both confirming and falsifying evidence, rather than only links already attached to a hypothesis. Separate independent criticism/owner adjudication from the original writer.

**Confidence calibration:** require probabilities of measurable events at defined horizons. Score resolved binary events with Brier score and, where suitable, log loss; use reliability bins, base-rate comparisons and uncertainty intervals. Keep narrative confidence separate from probability of excess return and probability of thesis confirmation. Report sample size and effective independent episodes by model/policy/action/regime. Unresolved outcomes remain unresolved, not zero. A few successful trades must not increase a model's investment authority.

### Bias and process-change safeguards

- **Look-ahead:** enforce `available_at <= decision_cutoff` at every evidence join; freeze World/portfolio/context versions; block live web/current caches in historical simulation. Distinguish event dates from release dates. Modern LLM historical reasoning remains hindsight-contaminated; use prospective shadow cohorts for the strongest evidence of live usefulness.
- **Data revisions:** append vintages instead of replacing values; maintain separate original-known and later-restated evaluations. FRED defaults generally expose what is known today; its real-time parameters/ALFRED support historical information sets. [Official FRED documentation](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html).
- **Survivorship:** archive universe membership, stable security identifiers, renames, inactive/delisted securities and corporate actions. Today's active assets and SPY/IWM constituents cannot define an old universe.
- **Selection:** preserve the full decision denominator, failures, missing inputs, declined recommendations and owner overrides; audit a random sample of discarded events against independent primary releases. Do not evaluate only researched winners or owner-executed ideas.
- **Overfitting:** preregister horizons, benchmarks, costs and promotion criteria; log every policy/model experiment, including rejected variants. Use chronological train/validation/untouched prospective evaluation, purge overlapping outcome windows, embargo as appropriate, cluster by episode and estimate uncertainty. Repeatedly testing the same history can manufacture apparent performance; use multiple-testing controls/PBO-style diagnostics when sample size permits. [Bailey et al., original research](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf).
- **Learning:** recommend one bounded process change per cohort review with a mechanism and falsifiable expected benefit. Shadow it against the unchanged baseline. Promote only after out-of-sample evidence and owner review; retain rollback. Improve source coverage, calibration, gating, timing or risk separately. Do not automatically rewrite old theses, benchmark definitions or forecast probabilities.
