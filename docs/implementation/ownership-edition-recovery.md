## Bounded publication

jobs of at most eight pairs. Each assessment uses the same complete immutable
manifest, reads complete reports, retains the full portfolio and comparison
evidence, and receives independent review with the existing single correction
limit. GPT-6.1 Sol remains the centrally configured model for generation,
correction and review. These are recommendation assessment jobs, not new company
or fund investigations; they do not reserve investigation capacity.

Job identities include owner, frozen manifest, assessment version and exact
pair scope. Retries reuse successful durable outputs. The renewal source must
belong to the same owner and have been published by the frozen cutoff, so a
restart cannot borrow a later conclusion or change its partition. Finalization
requires every prerequisite to succeed, verifies owner, scope, immutable hash
and review receipts, then reapplies coverage, cash, concentration and other
portfolio gates across the complete edition. Failed review or missing results
preserve the previous accepted edition rather than publishing partial coverage.

The private input projection preserves exact original evidence and the full
restored manifest. Large captured documents also receive deduplicated text
files with byte counts and SHA-256 hashes. Readable packet files retain all
structured facts, topic quotations and provenance, referring to complete text
without truncation. Relevant passages beyond document introductions remain
available. No report, frozen database record or source claim is rewritten.
