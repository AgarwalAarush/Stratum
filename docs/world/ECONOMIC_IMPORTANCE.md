# Economic importance and transmission

Importance is not headline popularity or stock-price movement. The system tests whether a change can propagate through institutions, physical capacity, financing, prices, or expectations.

Every promoted hypothesis must trace:

```text
event -> actor or physical change -> economic variable -> reach and duration
-> constraint or excess capacity -> adaptive responses and substitutes
-> possible rent recipient -> expectations question -> indicators and falsifiers
```

The specialist may identify plausible channels and missing evidence. It may not name an investment recommendation. The World Thinker may create a company research lead only after completing the chain. Host gates then require a verified active/tradable symbol, explicit capture mechanism, adequate evidence and transmission, deduplication, and daily capacity. A lead remains a question for independent company research.

Separate attention dimensions prevent a dramatic but economically isolated event from outranking a slower structural change. Uncertainty is not treated as negative importance: high-consequence uncertainty increases investigation priority.
