# World attention policy

Stratum is recall-first at the evidence boundary and selective at the active-World boundary.

## Non-deletion rule

Raw documents, feed items, normalized event history, attention decisions, weak signals, and their lineage are append-only evidence. A low attention route changes processing priority; it does not authorize deletion. Only rebuildable indexes and caches may be pruned.

## Source lanes

Every source is deterministically assigned to official/primary, global reporting, specialist, research/data, company disclosure, market commentary, PR/syndication, or community/discovery. Source families collapse syndicated copies for corroboration counts while preserving every URL.

FMP stock news is company intelligence by default. It crosses into World only for bankruptcy, M&A, shutdowns, regulation, sanctions, system-level capacity changes, active dependency exposure, or independently corroborated spillovers.

## Attention dimensions

Routing records evidence quality, novelty, magnitude, system reach, duration, propagation potential, transmission clarity, time sensitivity, active dependency, and uncertainty independently. The dimensions remain visible; they are never collapsed into a hidden master score.

Routes are urgent, investigate, monitor, awareness, company-only, and noise. Weak evidence with large possible consequences becomes an investigation. Official evidence is always retained. Aging and lane fairness guarantee an eligible item a triage opportunity within 24 hours.

## Agent boundary

Deterministic host code routes and validates. Zero to two read-only specialists assess selected events. The World Thinker alone proposes World changes. The critic checks the full proposal. Host code owns IDs, timestamps, source resolution, Git writes, and research gates. Company research is separate. Only the owner may accept a company thesis or make a capital decision.

## Tunable and fixed controls

Lane quotas and numeric routing thresholds may move by at most 10% in a version. Source trust, ontology, evidence gates, permissions, call caps, research limits, and investment boundaries cannot auto-change. A candidate policy shadows for seven complete days and promotes only when recall improves without unacceptable precision, latency, cost, hard-case, lineage, or safety regression.
