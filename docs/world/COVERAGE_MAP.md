# World coverage map

The four maintained specialist lenses are intentionally broad and non-exclusive:

1. Geopolitics and institutions: state power, conflict, law, authoritarianism, elections, sanctions, alliances, and institutional capacity.
2. Physical economy: climate, energy, resources, food, water, health, demographics, logistics, and supply chains.
3. Macro and finance: sovereigns, banking, credit, liquidity, inflation, currencies, rates, and market plumbing.
4. Technology and industrial capacity: compute, semiconductors, software, cyber, manufacturing, industrial policy, and bottlenecks.

The router chooses one primary lens and at most one cross-domain lens. Awareness items use none. The maintained lenses are retrieval aids, not fixed thesis templates; the Thinker may form hypotheses that cross or sit outside their usual examples.

Required historical benchmark families include Iran, China-Taiwan, authoritarianism, ENSO, sovereign and banking stress, export controls, AI power, routine earnings, PR syndication, contradictory reports, and novel cross-domain combinations.
